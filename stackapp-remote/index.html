Privacy Policy

StackApp Remote, by Martin Podt and Edwin Mooijaart. Last updated: November 15, 2026

StackApp Remote collects no personal data. It talks only to your camera, over a USB cable or the camera's own Wi-Fi, and sends nothing to us or anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What the App keeps stays on your device: its settings, the series files of your focus brackets (and small copies of the photos if you switch that on), your camera's Wi-Fi name and password for reconnecting, and a log for support. The phone's camera is only used to read your camera's QR code. Deleting the App deletes this data.
