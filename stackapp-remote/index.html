Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Martin Podt and Edwin Mooijaart ("we", "us") built StackApp Remote ("the App"), a remote control for focus bracketing with a camera, for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. This page explains what the App does with information.

Summary

The App talks only to your camera, over a USB cable or the camera's own Wi-Fi. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data, and it needs no account and no internet connection. Your photos stay on the camera's memory card and, where you ask for it, on your own device.

Data Collection & Transmission

  • No personal data, photos or usage data are sent to us, to any server, cloud service or third party.
  • No analytics, tracking, telemetry or advertising frameworks are used.
  • No user account is required or created.
  • The connection to the camera is local: a USB cable, or the camera's own Wi-Fi network between your device and the camera.

Permissions

  • Camera (of your phone or tablet): only to read the QR code your camera shows for its Wi-Fi connection. The image is processed on your device and not stored.
  • Local network: to reach the camera over its Wi-Fi.
  • USB: to control the camera over the cable.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App keeps the following on your own device; none of it is transmitted anywhere:

  • Settings of the App (such as the focus bracket, the language and the text size).
  • Series files describing each focus bracket (the focus steps, exposures and the names of the photos on the camera's card), and, only if you switch it on, small copies of the photos, in the App's Documents/StackApp folder.
  • The camera's Wi-Fi name and password, when you connect over Wi-Fi, so the App can connect again by itself.
  • A diagnostic log of what the App and the camera did. It stays on your device and is only shared with us if you choose to send it for support.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
