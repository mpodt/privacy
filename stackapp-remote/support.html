StackApp Remote: Support

StackApp Remote shoots a focus bracket with your Sony camera from your phone: press once, and the focus moves by itself from shot to shot. Stack the series in StackLab. It works with Sony cameras that offer PC Remote, such as the a7C and a7C II, on Android and iPhone.

Connecting

  • By USB, for the smoothest live view: on the camera MENU → Setup → USB Connection: PC Remote. Android: a USB-C cable to the camera, then allow access on the phone. iPhone with USB-C: a USB-C cable. iPhone with Lightning: Apple's Lightning to USB Camera Adapter and a USB cable (a plain USB-C to Lightning cable does not work).
  • By Wi-Fi: on the camera PC Remote Function → PC Remote: On (Wi-Fi Direct). In the App choose Over Wi-Fi with QR code and scan the code on the camera's screen, or type the camera's network name and password.
  • Only one app at a time can control the camera: close other camera apps first.

Common questions

  • The lens does not move during a focus bracket: set Auto Review to Off on the camera, and put the camera in MF (the lens switch, if there is one, on AF).
  • The live view stops for a few seconds over Wi-Fi: some cameras (such as the a7C) do this now and then; the App waits for the camera and says so on the live view. Over USB the live view runs smoothly.
  • A setting is grey: tap it, the App says why (often the camera's mode decides it).
  • The connection is lost: the App connects again by itself; if not, switch the camera off and on.
  • Where are my photos? On the camera's memory card. The series file for StackLab is in the App's Documents/StackApp folder: on Android in the phone's Documents/StackApp, on an iPhone in the Files app → On My iPhone → StackApp Remote.
