StackApp Remote – Support

StackApp Remote shoots a focus bracket with your camera: press once, and the focus moves by itself from shot to shot. Stack the series in StackLab.

Connecting

  • On the camera: MENU → Setup → USB Connection: PC Remote.
  • iPhone with USB-C: a USB-C cable to the camera. iPhone with Lightning: Apple's Lightning to USB Camera Adapter and a USB cable (a plain USB-C to Lightning cable does not work).
  • Only one app at a time can control the camera: close other camera apps first.

Common questions

  • The lens does not move during a focus bracket: set Auto Review to Off on the camera, and put the camera in MF (the lens switch, if there is one, on AF).
  • A setting is grey: tap it, the App says why (often the camera's mode decides it).
  • The connection is lost: the App connects again by itself; if not, switch the camera off and on.
  • Where are my photos? On the camera's memory card. The series file for StackLab is in the App's Documents/StackApp folder (Files app → On My iPhone → StackApp Remote).
