Privacy

Photo Tour NL · 15 November 2026

Photo Tour NL is the app for our photo workshop weeks. Martin Podt and Edwin Mooijaart are responsible for the data in it. We use it only to run the week you take part in.

What we keep

  • Your account: username, name, role, language and profile photo. We make it for you; we don't ask for your email or phone number. We can see the password on your login card until you choose your own.
  • What you share in the chats and daily notes: messages (translated automatically), photos, voice messages, documents, polls and likes.
  • Your location, only when you share it, for the time you choose.
  • Your push address, if you switch on notifications.

Who sees it

  • The others in your week see what you share in the group chat.
  • We, as admins, can see everything in the app, private chats included.
  • The QR code on your certificate shows your name and dates to whoever scans it.

Services we use

Supabase (database, in the EU), Cloudflare and Netlify (hosting), MyMemory (translation), the maps of OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap, PDOK, OpenTopoMap and NASA, the route planner FOSSGIS (your position rounded to about 100 m, for drive times) and your phone's push service. Some of them are in the United States. We don't share your data otherwise, don't sell it, and use no ads or tracking.

How long

When a week is no longer needed, we delete its chats, photos and shared locations. The program, the daily notes, your account and your certificate stay. We delete your account when you ask.

Your rights

We use your data to run the week you signed up for; your location and notifications only with your consent. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at info@martinpodt.com, and you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
