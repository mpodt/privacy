Privacy Policy

Photo Tour NL · last updated 15 November 2026

Martin Podt and Edwin Mooijaart ("we") run photo workshops in the Netherlands. Photo Tour NL ("the App") is the web app for the participants and course leaders of a workshop week, with the admin site we use to plan it. This page explains which personal data the App processes, why, who else receives it, and what your rights are. We are the controller of this data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Summary

We use your data only to run the workshop week you take part in. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking. We do not ask for your email address or phone number. Our database is in the EU.

Which data

  • Your account, which we create for you before the week: username, display name (also in Chinese), role (participant, course leader or admin), language, and a profile photo if one is added. The App also notes when you last had it open.
  • Your password. The password on your login card is kept by the App, so that we can show or print your card again; we, the admins, can see it. When you choose your own password, the card's password is removed, and your own password is stored only as a secure hash that nobody can read.
  • What you share in the group chat and in private chats: messages with their automatic translations, photos, voice messages, documents, polls and votes, reactions and replies; also the photos you pick for the daily notes, and likes. Photos are made smaller on your phone before they are uploaded, which also removes the location stored in them. Documents and voice messages are uploaded as they are.
  • What the others in your week see of you: your name and profile photo, what you share in the group chat, your likes, which messages you have read (read ticks), and, in a private chat, when you last had the App open.
  • Your location, only when you choose to share it:
    • A live location, for the time you choose (15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours): the group sees it on a map, or in a private chat only the other person. Afterwards the group map's copy is deleted; the message in the chat stays, with a position from that time.
    • A location you send as a message stays in the chat like any other message.
    • For the drive time to a place, your phone sends its position, rounded to about 100 metres, directly to a route planner (FOSSGIS, see below). We do not store it.
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, your browser's push address is stored, so that we can send you announcements and chat notifications. Switching them off removes it.
  • Your certificate: at the end of the week the App makes a certificate with your name (also in Chinese) and the dates you took part. Its QR code opens a public page that shows that name and those dates to anyone who scans it, so they can see that the certificate is real.
  • A forgotten password: if you ask for a new one on the login screen, we keep your username with the request.
  • On your phone: the App keeps your login, your settings and the week's program in your browser's storage, and keeps photos and map tiles you have seen, so that it also works with a poor connection.

What we can see as admins

We, the two admins, can see and manage the App's content, for example to delete a message. Private chats are not shown to us in the App, but technically we can get at them and at every uploaded file, for example by logging in to an account.

Why

To run the workshop week you signed up for (the program, communication within the group, sharing photos and notes, the certificate): the performance of our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Live location and notifications only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the App or on your phone.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase: database, login, file storage and live updates. Our project is in the EU.
  • Cloudflare hosts the App's web pages and, like any web host, sees your IP address. An older version of the App is still online at phototour-nl.netlify.app, hosted by Netlify, with the same database.
  • Translation: the text of chat messages (also in private chats) and the texts we write (daily notes, the program, places) go to a translation service, to translate between Chinese, Dutch and English. Now that is MyMemory (Translated, Italy); if we change it, we update this page.
  • Maps and routes: OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap, PDOK (the Dutch government's maps), OpenTopoMap and NASA (satellite images) receive the map area you look at and your IP address. The route planner of FOSSGIS (OpenStreetMap Germany) receives your rounded position for a drive time.
  • Weather: the forecast comes from our own service Bosweer (app.martinpodt.com), which sees your IP address.
  • Link previews: when a chat message contains a web link, our server opens that page to show a preview. The website sees our server, not you.
  • Notifications are delivered, encrypted, by your browser's push service (from Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft).
  • Navigate opens Amap, Apple Maps or Google Maps with the place's location; from then on that app's own privacy rules apply. Links to Bosweer Lite, martinpodt.com and the focus stacking calculator open those websites.
  • Backups: at the moment no separate backup is made. When we switch it on, an encrypted copy of the database will be kept for 30 days in our private repository at GitHub.

Some of these services are based in the United States or work worldwide: Cloudflare, Netlify, GitHub, the push services, and NASA. They receive only what is described above. We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

How long

  • When a week is no longer useful to the group, we clear it: its chat (messages, photos, voice messages, documents, reactions), the photos of the day, the read ticks and the shared locations are then deleted for good, files included. We do this ourselves; it does not happen by itself.
  • After that the program, the daily notes, your account and your certificate stay, so that the certificate can still be checked.
  • A live location on the group map is deleted soon after the time you chose.
  • Your account is deleted when you ask us to.
  • Backups, once switched on: 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a common format, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us (below). You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

When the App changes what it does with your data, we update this page and its date.
