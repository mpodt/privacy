Privacy

Photo Tour NL · 15 November 2026

Who

Martin Podt and Edwin Mooijaart, info@martinpodt.com.

What and why

Your account (username, name, role, language, photo) and what you share in the app, to run the workshop week you signed up for: our agreement with you. Without an account you cannot use the app. Your location and notifications only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the app.

Who receives it

The others in your week; whoever scans the QR code on your certificate (your name and dates); and our service providers for hosting, translation, maps and notifications. Some of them are in the United States.

How long

When a week is no longer needed, we delete its chats, photos and locations. Your account and certificate stay until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to get a copy in a common format: info@martinpodt.com. You can also complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
