Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Martin Podt and Edwin Mooijaart ("we", "us") run photo workshops in the Netherlands. Photo Tour NL ("the App") is the web app for the participants and course leaders of a workshop week, and the admin site we use to plan it. This page explains which personal data the App processes, why, and what your rights are. We are the controller of this data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Summary

We only use your data to run the workshop week you take part in: your program, the group chat and the photos and notes you share. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking. The data is stored in the EU.

Which data

  • Your account, which we create for you before the week: username, display name (also in Chinese), your role (participant, course leader or admin), your language and, if you add one, a profile photo. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our database provider; we cannot read it.
  • What you share in the App: chat messages (with their automatic translations), photos, voice messages, documents and polls you post, the photos you pick for the daily notes, and likes.
  • Your location, only when you choose to:
    • When you share your location in a chat, the others in that chat see it on a map for the time you choose.
    • For the drive time to a location, your phone's position, rounded to about 100 metres, is sent to a route planner (see below). It is not stored by us.
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, your browser's push address is stored, so we can send you announcements and chat notifications.
  • On your phone: the App keeps your login and the day's program in your browser's storage, so it also works with a poor connection.

Why

To run the workshop week you signed up for (planning, communication within the group, sharing photos and notes): the performance of our agreement with you. Location and notifications only with your consent, which you can switch off at any time in the App or on your phone.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database, login, file storage, live updates), in the EU.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the App's web pages.
  • A translation service (now MyMemory) receives the text of chat messages to translate them between Chinese, Dutch and English.
  • Map and route services (OpenFreeMap, PDOK, OpenTopoMap, and OpenStreetMap's route planner FOSSGIS) receive the map area you look at, or your rounded position for a drive time, and like any website your IP address.
  • Your browser's push service (from Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers notifications.
  • An encrypted backup of the database is made every night.

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

How long

We keep the data of a workshop week for as long as it is useful to the group after the week (for example to see the photos and notes again), and then clear the week. Your account is removed when you ask for it, or when you no longer take part in our workshops. Encrypted backups are kept for a limited time and then overwritten.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a common format. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
